Your entire nursing program, organized.

NurseOS is a free app for iPhone, iPad and Mac that turns one pasted Canvas link into a complete semester: every deadline and exam on one calm screen, clinical compliance dates computed ahead of time, and study tools built from the student's own notes. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to maintain.

SETUP — 60 SECONDS, ONCE
01

Find the calendar link in Canvas

Log in to Canvas on a computer, open Calendar, and click Calendar Feed in the bottom-right corner. A long link appears — it ends in .ics.

https://canvas.school.edu/feeds/calendars/user_kx82m4…ics
02

Copy it

The whole link, once. This link is personal to the student — it doesn't need a password and can't be used to log in as them.

03

Paste it into NurseOS

The app asks for it on first launch. Paste, and the semester appears: courses, deadlines, exams, all kept current automatically from then on.

✓  That's the entire setup. From tomorrow morning, one screen answers the only question that matters: am I okay for today?

PRIVACY — THE ANSWERS PARENTS ASK

Does my student need an account?

No. No sign-up, no login, no password to lose. The app works the moment it's installed.

Where does their data go?

Nowhere. Grades, documents and schedules stay on the student's devices and in their own iCloud, encrypted by Apple. NurseOS has no server and receives nothing.

What about the AI features?

Study tools run entirely on the device. Notes and materials are never uploaded to anyone, including us.

What does it cost?

The organizer is free, forever. Optional AI study tools are a small in-app purchase — never required.
