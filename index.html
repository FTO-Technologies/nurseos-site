04 — FOR CLINICAL COORDINATORS
THE RULE, SHOWN

Clinical eligibility, computed — not chased.

Every program loses clinical hours to expired paperwork. NurseOS reads the document a student drops in, extracts the dates, applies the renewal rule, and shows its work — so the student trusts the deadline instead of ignoring it.

Reminders begin 30 days out and escalate gently. No portal logins, no spreadsheet from the coordinator's office, no expired TB test on the morning of clinicals.

NurseOS reads the standard calendar feed that Canvas, Blackboard, Brightspace and Moodle all publish. If your program's LMS can export a calendar link — nearly all can — setup is the same single paste.

No. There is no sign-up, no login, and no NurseOS server. The app stores everything in the student's own iCloud, so it works the moment it's installed.

On the student's devices and in their personal iCloud, encrypted by Apple. Nothing is sent to us — we couldn't read a student's grades if we wanted to. AI features run entirely on-device.

The app is free to download and use. There is no institutional contract, no per-seat license, and nothing for a program to procure — you can simply recommend it at orientation.
